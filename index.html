Install in a minute

get gist on your phone.

Gist isn't on the Play Store yet. You install it straight from us as an APK file.

  1. Download the APK

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download. The file comes from Gist's official GitHub release.

  2. Allow the install

    Android will ask whether your browser can install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.

  3. Turn on Bank SMS

    Open Gist, allow SMS access, and tap Import last 90 days. Your Today screen fills up right away.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Installing from here gets you the same app, and you can move to the Play Store version later if one comes.

What does Gist read?

Your bank alert SMS, so it can log spends without you typing them in. You can also import a file or add a spend by hand.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Gist needs no account, and your ledger is encrypted and stays on your phone. Answers in Ask are worked out on your phone too.

How do I get updates?

Download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays where it is.
